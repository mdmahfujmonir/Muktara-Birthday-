The Story So Far

Some People Stay Important

Time and distance have a way of quietly rearranging who we talk to every day. That's normal. It doesn't rewrite who mattered.

🗓️

We haven't talked much lately. Life got busy, days turned into months, and somehow that's just what happened. No drama, no falling out — just time doing what time does.

🌟

But some people don't need daily conversation to still count. You're one of the ones I never stopped being glad I know, even on the days I forget to say it.

🎈

So today isn't about catching up on everything we missed. It's simpler than that — I just wanted you to know that someone out here remembered your birthday, and meant it.

😄

Also, fair warning: this website has jokes. Bad ones. I regret nothing. Happy Birthday Detected. Smile Loading. Achievement Unlocked: Another Awesome Year.

A Letter

For Muktara

Dear Muktara,

I'll be honest — I sat here for a bit trying to figure out how to start this without it sounding either too formal or too dramatic. So I'll just say it plainly: happy birthday. I hope today treats you exactly the way you deserve to be treated, which is very well.

We haven't kept in touch the way we used to, and if I'm being fair, that's on both of us — nobody's fault, just life pulling us in different directions. But every so often you cross my mind, usually followed by a memory that makes me smile before I even know why. That's a pretty good sign someone still matters.

I'm not going to pretend this is a grand romantic gesture, because it isn't — you're my friend, and this is just me making sure that on one specific day of the year, you get reminded that you're appreciated, thought of, and (this part is important) allowed to eat cake without guilt today. Calories don't count on your birthday. That's science. I checked. Twice.

So however this year has gone, however far apart we've drifted — I hope it's been kind to you, and I hope the year ahead is kinder. You don't need to reply to this. You don't need to explain the silence. Just smile for a second, maybe roll your eyes at how extra this website is, and then go have a good day.

Happy Birthday, Muktara. Genuinely.

— Monir
